Pricing Terminology

Cover Price
The standard retail price printed on the comic book by the publisher (e.g., ₹399 / $3.99).
Guide Price
The market value of a vintage or rare back-issue comic, determined by its current condition and demand.
Subscription Discount
A special 10% price reduction applied automatically to all titles added to your Pull List box.
